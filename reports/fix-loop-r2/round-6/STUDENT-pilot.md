## Things that were slightly confusing in the lesson (not blocking, minor notes)

- The lesson bundles K01–K05 and S01–S06 into one long scrolling page with no in-page anchors/table of contents visible in the rendered view; jumping back to re-check a specific block (e.g., re-reading S05 while answering q-saa-1-1-s05-mr) meant scrolling through the whole lesson again. A quick-nav or collapsible sections would help a student like me who wants to spot-check one block at a time.
- S06's AD Connector vs. AWS Managed Microsoft AD vs. per-account SAML comparison is presented as a dense block of four bullet options; it took me two reads to keep straight which one requires domain controllers in AWS. Not a factual problem, just a lot to hold in short-term memory in one paragraph.
- K04 lists five different IAM hygiene/analysis tools (customer managed policies, permissions boundary, Access Analyzer policy generation, last accessed info, Access Analyzer unused access findings, Trusted Advisor key rotation, external access findings) in one block — as a non-expert I found it easy to mix up "Access Analyzer policy generation" with "Access Analyzer unused access findings" on first read; the exam tip at the end was what actually clarified it for me.

None of these confusions affected my ability to answer correctly — the exam tips at the end of each block consistently resolved ambiguity before I got to the drills.
